# Combat page

The sheet's combat mode, laid out like Roll20's character page. Only the layout and content structure are copied, not Roll20's look.

## Files Covered

| File | Purpose |
|------|---------|
| `turn.ts` | Pure: `turnActions` sorts what a character can do into the five action groups (spells, resources, features, pact weapon) |
| `combat.ts` | Pure: `spellAttackRows` (known library spells, typed-in quick-cast spells, and cantrips from the race, feats or items that attack or force a save; innate cantrips use the ability their note names, else the casting ability, else the best of INT/WIS/CHA for non-casters), `withRollFromText` (save/attack from the text when the library has none), `addAction` / `updateAction` / `removeAction` / `actionsOf` (the player's own actions), `actionUses` / `spendAction` (their uses and Use), `resolveDice` (fills STR…CHA, PROF, LEVEL), `actionRoll` (an action's attack bonus or save DC and dice), `isHealing`, `effectToggles` (Rage, Sneak Attack, Hex, Hunter's Mark, Divine Favor), `masteryLimit` |
| `LiveMap.tsx` | `useLiveCombat` (the campaign's live combat from `src/combat/live`), `LiveMapTab` (Map tab: round, whose turn, the map with tokens, move your own token, full screen with the HP chip), `CombatStartedNotice` |
| `liveMap.ts` | Pure: `mapStatus`, `ownToken`, `bringsCharacter`, `canMoveTo`, `tokenHealth`. Tested in `liveMap.test.ts` |
| `library.ts` | `useLibrary`: the character's edition of the content library (spells, features, traits, feats, equipment, classes, subclasses); empty while loading. Also used by `src/print` |
| `featureTexts.ts` | `useFeatureTexts`: names and rules text of class features, racial traits, feats and the background feature in the current language (our summary first in Hebrew), plus languages, tools and the SRD attribution. Shared by `FeaturesTraits` and the printable sheet |
| `CombatPage.tsx` | `CharacterCard` (portrait and level, class and subclass, race, background, XP, AC, speed, proficiency, passive perception, Level Up, Initiative roll, and the sheet's HP and Rest cards), `AbilitiesPanel` with Mod and Save rolls, `SkillsPanel` (all three shared with story mode), `CombatTabs`, `Fight` (Combat tab), `FeaturesTraits` |
| `combat.css` | Styles, logical properties for RTL. The top part (character card, abilities, skills) is shared with story mode; the combat-only part (tabs, attacks, roll lines, effects switches, actions, action form) follows the Liquid Glass standard in `src/design/DESIGN.md`, with glass tokens only so high contrast and Reduce transparency turn it solid. The DM screen reuses the tab bar |
| `combat.test.ts` | Helper tests, and checks that the combat-only styles use glass tokens, logical properties and high-contrast edges |

## Rules

- **Map tab** (`LiveMap.tsx`): while the DM runs a live combat for the campaign in play (`party_encounters_public`, see `src/combat/COMBAT.md`), `sheetLayout({ liveMap })` adds a Map tab after Combat, with a dot until opened and a notice strip that opens it. A player may move only the token of the character they bring (`bringsCharacter`), tap-to-select then tap-the-destination; `canMoveTo` checks first and `moveToken` asks the server, which can still refuse (locked, not yours, off the map). Monsters show only healthy, bloodied or down; party tokens show HP. The tab goes when the combat ends. The stage is a simple SVG grid until the shared `BattleMap` lands.
- Tabs render existing sheet sections (`COMBAT_TABS` in `../mode.ts`); add a card to a tab there, don't duplicate it here. Phones use `PHONE_COMBAT_TABS` (adds Checks with abilities and skills) and a sticky `.cs-tabbar` with the HP chip. Nothing on the page shows twice: no Spells card or quick-cast bar beside the tabs, since the Spells tab has the slots and spells and the Combat tab rolls them.
- Every tap rolls through `useSheetRolls`, so results reach the dice history. A leveled spell in the attacks table spends the lowest free slot through `apply` (undoable) and rolls at that level.
- Every roll result is labeled with what it is for (To hit with the AC it reaches, Damage with its type, the check or save), never shown bare.
- Roll results sit in a `FadingResult` (`src/dice/ui`): they fade after 8 s, a tap or sideways swipe closes them now, hovering, pressing or keyboard focus holds them, and a new roll replaces the card. Every roll stays in the dice button's history.
- Actions, Bonus Actions, Reactions, Free Actions and Other are filled in from the character (`turn.ts` `turnActions`, rendered by `TurnRows`): spells by casting time with Cast (action spells that attack or force a save stay in the attacks table, which now holds only action spells; item and race spells spend their own use), resources by `featureActionKind` with Use (unlisted ones go under Other; ki, focus and sorcery points stay on their cards), features without uses as reminders (Cunning Action, Martial Arts) or with Use when they spend a pool (Flurry of Blows rolls its unarmed strikes; monks always get Flurry, Patient Defense and Step of the Wind rows, free with a Boost button for 1 Focus in 2024; Stunning Strike shows its DC) or have a `FEATURE_TEMPLATES` entry (Dark One's Blessing: "Dropped a foe" gives CHA + warlock level temp HP), and Pact of the Blade's Summon (one of your melee weapons, or a conjured one, becomes the equipped pact weapon: `summonPactWeapon`). Feature names open their text. The player's own actions (`Character.customActions`) follow as extra rows. An action can carry a roll (an attack roll using an ability + proficiency, or a save DC of 8 + proficiency + modifier, then dice such as `1d8+CHA`) shown as a 🎲 button, an effect: extra damage, stat changes (the item effects editor: AC, saves, checks, weapon attack and damage, initiative, spell attack and DC, speed, ability scores) or both, and limited `uses` (a number, PB or a modifier per short rest, long rest or dawn; resource `act:<id>`, refilled by rests, shown as `left/max` on the row and not on the Resources card). An action with uses or an effect gets Use instead of 🎲: `spendAction` spends a use (none left warns, never blocks), switches the effect on, then rolls. Its effect switch sits on its own row, not under Effects, so it shows once; the switch is saved on the action (`active`), and while on `src/rules/itemEffects.ts` counts its stat changes like an equipped item's. A filled-in row with the same name as one of the player's actions is hidden.
- A roll result lists the switched-on effects that went into it (`effectsUsed`): dice riders that were rolled and only the stat changes that touch that roll (weapon: attack, damage, the attack's ability; spell: spell attack or DC; action: its ability).
- Effects (`../components/Effects.tsx`, `EffectRows`): Rage and Sneak Attack are switches kept in component state. Spells are saved running effects (`state.effects`): every known or pinned spell whose effect changes numbers (Hex, Bless, Shield of Faith...) has a switch even before it is cast (switching it on starts it without a slot), and whatever is running shows with concentration, slot level, duration and ✕ End. Switching a running effect off keeps it tracked (cast on an ally) without its numbers. Spell damage rides on weapon attacks and, unless `weaponOnly`, spell attacks; a 2014 smite (`once`) is spent by the hit. Story mode shows the same rows (minus Rage and Sneak Attack, which sit on its Attacks card) as its own Effects card.
- Casting an attack or save spell from the attacks table also starts its running effect, so concentration (Hold Person) is tracked and a new concentration spell ends the old one.
- Feature, trait, feat and spell text comes from the content library for the character's edition. The Features tab shows the source's CC-BY attribution: keep it.
- Class and subclass feature text goes through `featureDescription` in `combat.ts`. A subclass feature only uses the SRD entry of that same subclass, then its own `why.subfeature.<subclassId>.<name>` summary, because subclasses share names with different rules ("Bonus Proficiencies"). `combat.test.ts` fails if two subclasses show the same text for a shared name, unless `SAME_RULES` lists them.
- "Level Up" opens the edit flow (`onEdit`).
