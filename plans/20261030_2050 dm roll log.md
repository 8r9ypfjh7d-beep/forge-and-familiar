# DM roll log (2026-10-30 20:50)

Ziv: "I want the DM to be able to see all the dice rolls of all the players."

## What exists
- Party page: `RollFeed` (public sheet rolls of brought characters, live from `ff.rolls.v1.*` and Supabase `rolls`) and `SecretRolls` DM log (`secret_rolls`). Both only on the Party tab, two separate panels, no filters.
- The DM screen never showed rolls.

## Plan
1. `src/dm/rolls.ts` (pure): `mergeDmRolls` joins public and secret rolls into one newest-first log, `filterRolls` (player + kind: all / attacks / checks / saves / secret), `playerSummary` (count, last roll, nat 20s, nat 1s per player), `unseenCount` for the tab badge.
2. `src/dm/RollsTab.tsx`: Rolls tab on the DM screen. Player chips (tap to filter), kind segmented control, unified live log grouped by action (attack + damage as one card), secret rolls tagged, Clear secret rolls behind a ConfirmDialog. `useDmRolls` keeps it live (own history, other tabs, Supabase realtime for `rolls` and `secret_rolls`).
3. Tab badge: rolls since the DM last opened the tab (`ff.dm.rollsSeen.v1.<userId>`).
4. Without a campaign in play the tab explains how to pick one.
5. Party page keeps its feed (players use it).
